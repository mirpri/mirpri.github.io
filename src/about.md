I am an undergraduate student in the Turing Class at the School of Computer Science and Technology, Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST). My interests lie broadly in AI systems, with a particular focus on efficient inference for large models, agent systems, and the interaction between machine learning and systems.

I am especially interested in how emerging AI workloads challenge the design of existing serving systems. My current work explores ways to make large-model inference more adaptive and efficient by taking advantage of workload structure, execution dynamics, and model behavior. More broadly, I am interested in systems that can better support increasingly complex AI applications, especially those involving agents, multimodal models, and long-running workflows.

I also enjoy building practical systems and turning ideas into working applications. My experience spans AI-powered applications, full-stack development, and system-level engineering, and I like working across the boundary between research prototypes and real-world products.

Beyond research and development, I am actively involved in technical communities at HUST, where I participate in collaborative projects. I am always interested in learning about new ideas in AI systems and working with people who enjoy building things that are both technically interesting and useful.
